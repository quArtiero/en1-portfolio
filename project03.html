Project 3: Biomimicry

Bio-Inspired Structure/Function Analysis

The project was to pick an animal, figure out the structure-function relationship behind something it does, and rebuild that in LEGO. I picked a seagull and went for the wing flap. Solo build, motors only.

Each wing is two long beams running side by side out of the body, a joint partway out where the wing changes angle, and a thin rod carrying the rest of the span to the tip. Two motors behind the hub drive everything.

The flapping was the hard part. A gull's wing is bent and the bend changes through the stroke, but every LEGO piece is rigid and straight. So I broke the wing into segments meeting at an angle. Enough straight pieces at angles starts to look like a curve.

There was an extra challenge too, replicating how the animal senses and reacts with a SPIKE Prime sensor. I didn't get to it. This one's motors only.
