Project 1: Getting to Know You

For this LEGO build I made a fishing rod. I have always loved going out fishing; it brings me peace. When you're fishing there is no stress on your mind. Growing up it was the thing I always wanted to be doing, and most of my "important" life decisions were made while I was fishing.

Because I was short on pieces and colors, the rod ended up pink and blue. It has a reel that is as functional as I could make it, a hook that is the very definition of "do what you can with what you have," and a base that looks more like wreckage than a stand.
