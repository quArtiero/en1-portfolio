Activity 1: Drink Coaster

Use Generative AI to design a drink coaster for a client.

We had to design a drink coaster for a client, using AI the whole way: to brainstorm the interview questions, to simulate the interview itself, and to generate the final design. I worked with Faisal in Class Group #13.

We started with the questions. The goal was to learn enough about the person that the design could only belong to them, so we mixed basic facts anyone could answer with the weirder stuff that's harder to dig up. Then we set up Claude Opus 5 to play our client, told it how to answer, and ran the interview in character. At the end we asked it to summarize the conversation and pulled our design criteria from that.

The coaster came out with a heavy, forged look: worn bronze, rough chipped edge, like it's been knocked around for years. The face has a long-bearded wizard playing electric guitar next to an amp, lightning bolts and stars around him, and a guitar pick sitting in a channel along one edge. Two engraved lines: "ABSURDLY DENSE. LIKE A METEOR." around the rim, and "THAT DENT IS A DIARY." under it.

What surprised me was how much the emotional side drove the design. I expected the concrete facts to be the useful part, but how the person feels mattered way more than I thought. How we feel is very important.

Tools used: Claude Opus 5 for the interview (good reasoning, stays objective) and Gemini 3.1 Pro for the image.
