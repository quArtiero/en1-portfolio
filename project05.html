Project 5: Spirograph Drawing Machine

Create geometric designs with two rotating motors.

The brief was to draw with two motors. A pen rides at the end of a linked arm, each link turned by its own motor, and the shape it traces comes out of the relationship between the two speeds. The card asks the same machine from two sides: as an artist, which designs actually look good — simple, complex, or wild; as a mathematician, which variables change the drawing (speed, arm length), and whether you can predict the output from the inputs.

I built the arm out of two beams that meet at a joint, both driven from the hub end. The marker is strapped to the far end with rubber bands so it rests its own weight on the paper, and the paper is clipped flat to two baseplates underneath, so the pen always has a fixed sheet to work on while the arm swings.

I ran both motors continuously at speeds that are close but not equal: 210 and 200 degrees per second, a ratio of 21 to 20. That one-part-in-twenty difference is the whole pattern. At matched speeds the pen would retrace a single loop forever; slightly off, every pass lands a little rotated from the one before, so the loops walk around the centre instead of closing on themselves. The two motors only come back into step after 21 turns of one and 20 of the other — about 36 seconds at those speeds — and anything past that draws over the same path again.

I let it draw for about 49 seconds — the video at the foot of the sheet is the whole run — one full 36-second cycle and a third of another, the pen already going back over its own ring. That is what the sheet shows: the loops close into a ring, the middle is inked almost solid, and one small gap survives at the centre where the pen never reached.

Still to add: whether I tried the challenge (varying the speed difference without changing the code).

Code:

# Setup: spirograph motors are connected to ports C and D.
from hub import port
import runloop
import motor

async def main():
    # Run the motors continuously at a close 21:20 speed ratio.
    motor.run(port.C, 210)
    motor.run(port.D, 200)

    # Keep the spirograph drawing until you stop the program.
    while True:
        await runloop.sleep_ms(10)

runloop.run(main())
