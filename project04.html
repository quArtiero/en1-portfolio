Project 4: Top Spinner

Build a top — and spin it.

The brief was to build a top and spin it. The card framed it from two sides: as a physicist, which properties make a top spin longest and stay stable; as an engineer, how to raise its circular momentum and which gear ratios get it turning fastest.

I went after speed. I used every gear size in the set and built them into a compound train: each stage steps from a larger gear down to a smaller one, and that smaller gear shares an axle with the larger gear of the next stage, so the speed multiplies from stage to stage instead of just once. The large motor drives the first stage; the last one spins the top.
