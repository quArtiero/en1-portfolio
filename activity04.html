Activity 4: Haunted House Prep

Pick one of the haunted-house prep tasks, build it, and document it.

In-class activity: pick one of the haunted-house prep tasks and build it. The scares need machinery, and the task sheet points at a real problem with drops: a motor can wind something up, but letting it back down at motor speed is slow, and slow isn't scary.

I went after that problem with a clutch. The motor winds the skeleton up through a pulley, and on the trigger the motor disengages from the pulley, so the skeleton comes down in free fall to scare a visitor.

Still to add: how the clutch works, what triggers it, how the runs went, and the photos.
