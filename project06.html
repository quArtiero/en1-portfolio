Project 6: Simple Car

Build a sturdy car with as few pieces as possible — forward, backward, and spin.

The brief ran the other way from every build so far: a sturdy car out of as few pieces as possible, able to drive forward, backward, and spin. The card frames it twice — as an engineer, which connections are structurally sound; as an economist, what can come off the build without the car coming apart.

The driving task was a whole repertoire, not just making it move: fast forward and slow forward, fast backward and slow backward, a small arc and a big arc, and a turn on the car’s own axis done slow and then fast.

Still to add: what I built and how few pieces it took, the code behind each maneuver, whether it survived the knee-height drop test, and the photos.
